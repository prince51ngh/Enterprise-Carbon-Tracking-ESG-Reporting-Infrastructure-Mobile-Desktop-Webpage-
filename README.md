🌿 Welcome to VerdeLedger! 🚀 This full-stack web application is designed to help organizations seamlessly bridge the gap between financial stability and environmental responsibility. By providing automated reporting pipelines and intuitive dashboards, VerdeLedger makes balancing bottom-line growth and carbon tracking actionable and clear. 🌍⚖️

Forget fragmented spreadsheets and siloed ESG tools! VerdeLedger treats environmental data with the exact same precision and auditability applied to traditional financial statements—reducing compliance reporting cycles from weeks to hours! ⏳✨

✨ Key Features & Capabilities
🏭 Scope 1–3 Carbon Logic
Real-time tracking of heavy machinery, facility utility logs, and fleet operations. The system automatically ingests and normalizes data, parsing supply chain parameters natively to construct verifiable footprint models. 🔗 Includes secure API integrations for supplier-level Scope 3 data aggregation.

💰 Capital Ledger Integration
Map your carbon emissions directly to capital expenditure and operational costs! 📉 Provides executive-level dashboards that forecast the financial impact of carbon reduction scenarios, helping CFOs balance growth targets with environmental mandates. 📈

📋 Global Compliance & Standards
Generate exportable, audit-ready trails perfectly formatted for global regulatory submissions. 🌍 Built to align out-of-the-box with ISSB, GHG Protocol, and GRI frameworks, ensuring you are always prepared for reporting cycles. 📑✅

🧮 Interactive Carbon Estimator
A built-in dynamic tool allowing users to generate a preliminary operational carbon output index approximation based on facility energy usage (kWh) and fleet run-time! ⚡ Get immediate feedback tiers (🟢 Low, 🟡 Moderate, 🔴 High) and actionable optimization recommendations.
